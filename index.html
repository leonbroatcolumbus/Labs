Agent in a Day

Lab guides

Work through these in order. Your presenter will start each one from the front, then hand over.

Lab 1
Build your first agent
Agent Builder, grounded in a public reference source. Ends with a question it cannot answer.
Lab 2
Give it what only you have
Copilot Studio. The plant's own documents, and a tool that writes a maintenance request.
Lab 3
The first join in the chain
A specialist child agent, and knowing when to hand over rather than act.
After Lab 3
Watch it work on its own
No building. Two demonstrations, then a conversation about your own idea.
A note on the later use cases. Each lab has extra use cases we do not run on the day. They are left in deliberately — work through them in your own time. Some keep Microsoft's own Contoso scenario rather than Vestbjerg, and some need a licence this training tenant does not have.
